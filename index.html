About Us

Skills for a Better Destiny — Excellence in Technical and Vocational Education

RP NGOMA College (Rwanda Polytechnic - Ngoma College) is a premier polytechnic institution dedicated to practical technical education, applied research, and innovation. Our campus equips students with hands-on engineering, ICT, and hospitality skills to solve real-world industry challenges.

Whether you are pursuing Diplomas, Advanced Diplomas, or professional short courses in software engineering, civil engineering, and cloud automation, RP NGOMA College provides the mentorship, modern workshops, and industry partnerships you need to succeed.
